Key Features

Find the member

Search by phone number in a second — or register a new one at the counter.

Enter the amount. Points are awarded automatically: Bronze 1 point per ₹10, Silver 1.5 points per ₹10, Gold 2 points per ₹10, and Platinum 0.3 points per ₹1.

Bronze → Silver (500) → Gold (2000) → Platinum (5000) as lifetime points grow.

4

Redeem rewards

100 points = ₹10 off. Balance updates instantly at checkout.
